HOW TO PLAY
WAKE-RUNNER OPERATIONS PROTOCOL

◈ FLIGHT CONTROL

  • WASD / ARROWS Vector thrust
  • MOUSE Turret tracking aim
  • LMB / SPACE Plasma cannons (hold)

◈ TACTICAL ABILITIES

  • SHIFT Chrono-Dodge — bullet-time + phase shields
  • E / RMB Quantum EMP — stuns & purges bullets
  • Q OVERDRIVE — ultimate weapon surge

◈ COMBAT DOCTRINE

  • GRAZE Near-miss bullets for score + energy
  • COMBO Chain kills to raise your rank D→X
  • XP Collect cyan cores to level up

◈ THREAT DATABASE

  • SEEKERS — swarm drones, fast & fragile
  • TURRETS — charge locking beam lances
  • BATTLESHIPS — heavy spread fire
  • KAMIKAZE — suicide divers, detonate at range
  • SPLITTERS — fragment into hunter packs
  • JUGGERNAUTS — elite shielded fortresses

◈ SURVIVAL TIPS

  • Your hull hitbox is smaller than the ship — thread the needle
  • Gold-rimmed ELITES drop tactical supplies — prioritize them
  • Bosses expose glowing weakpoint pods — destroy for bonus damage
  • Every 5th wave is a TITAN-CLASS boss encounter

◈ SUPPLY DROPS

  • REPAIR — restores hull plating
  • CELL — recharges barrier
  • MAGNET — vacuums all XP cores
  • PULSAR — damages every hostile on screen
  • STAR — bonus score cache
